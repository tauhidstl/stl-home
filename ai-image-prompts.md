# Home 4 — AI Image Prompt Reference

Companion prompt set for every photographic image slot on `home-4.html`, organized in on-page order.
Use with Midjourney, DALL·E, Firefly, or similar.

**Shared style DNA** (append to every prompt for visual consistency):
`photorealistic editorial photography, natural daylight or soft studio light, muted professional palette of slate grey, warm neutrals and charcoal with a restrained amber/orange accent, enterprise infrastructure & data-systems documentary tone, high detail, 35mm lens look, shallow depth of field where noted, no on-image text, no logos, no watermark, no visible human or animal faces — subjects shown from behind, in silhouette, cropped at the shoulders/neck, hands-only, or at a distance where features aren't discernible, no robots, no holograms, no futuristic HUD/sci-fi overlays, no cartoon or illustrated style, no cheap-looking stock-photo staging`

**Excluded from this list (already real, non-AI assets — do not regenerate):**
client-provided testimonial headshots, the 5 award/certification marks, the ~40 client/partner logos, the 7 success-story product screenshots, the Streams Tech wordmark/icon, and the current hero background video + poster (client-supplied).
